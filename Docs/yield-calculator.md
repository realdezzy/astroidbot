# Yield & Strategy Calculator

The **Yield Calculator** helps you project potential compound returns and strategy yields when deploying automated bots (Grid, DCA, AI Agents) over customizable time horizons.

---

## How It Works

1. **Select Asset**: Choose your target currency (USD, STX, SOL, ETH).
2. **Set Initial Investment**: Enter your starting capital (e.g. `$1,000` or `$10,000`).
3. **Choose Risk Profile**:
   - **Conservative**: Lower volatility assets, tight grid ranges, focus on capital preservation (~12-18% projected annual yield).
   - **Moderate**: Balanced DCA and active grid rebalancing across major tokens (~24-36% projected annual yield).
   - **Aggressive**: Active AI trading agents, token sniping, and dynamic range adjustments (~48-72% projected annual yield).
4. **Select Time Horizon**: Model returns across 30, 90, 180, or 365 days.

---

## Interactive Projection

The Yield Calculator displays:
- **Projected Total Value**: Estimated balance at the end of the duration.
- **Estimated Net Gain**: Total projected profit in selected currency.
- **Compound Breakdowns**: Monthly compounding breakdown visualization.

*Note: Yield projections are calculated based on historical strategy performance indicators and market volatility models. Past performance does not guarantee future results.*
